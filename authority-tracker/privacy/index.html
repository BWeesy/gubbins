BWeesy's Authority Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

BWeesy's Authority Tracker is an Android app for keeping score in deck-building games. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app keeps

The app saves the game in progress (each player's authority and the undo history) and your sound setting, so they are still there next time you open it. These are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere, are not included in device backups, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No internet access: the app works entirely offline and does not ask for network permission.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact
